# Agentic PMS — Employee Manual

**Mindgate Performance Management System**
For everyone who has been given a login. Version of 29 September 2026.

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Open the address your HR team circulated with your login.
2. Enter **your work email** and the **password HR gave you**.
3. Press **Sign in**.

**The first time you sign in, the system will stop and ask you to choose your
own password.** This is not optional and there is no way past it — the screen
says *"Choose your own password"* and nothing else will open until you have.
Your new password must be at least 8 characters. Pick something only you know:
the password HR issued was visible to whoever sent it to you.

To change it again later, click **Password** in the top-right corner of any
screen.

**If you cannot sign in**, check the email address first — the account is
keyed on the exact address in the employee master. If that is right and the
password still fails, ask HR to reset it. There is no self-service "forgot
password" link.

## 2. Finding your way around

Along the top you will see a row of coloured tabs. **Most employees see only
one: `Self`.** If you manage people you will also see `Manager`; department
heads see `Delivery Head`; HR sees `HR`. You only ever see the tabs your
account is entitled to — a tab you cannot use does not appear at all.

Under the tab is the menu for that tab. Everything in this manual lives under
**Self**.

| Menu item | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your landing page: what you owe, and what is waiting on someone else. |
| **My KRAs** | Your goals for the year, and where you write them. |
| **My Growth** | Your target achievements for the year, and the role you are aiming at. |
| **Quarterly Connects** | Your quarterly conversations with your manager. |
| **Mid-Year Review** | Your own mid-year checkpoint against each KRA. |
| **Annual Review** | Your self-appraisal at the end of the year. |
| **Final Rating** | The consolidated rating once the cycle closes. |
| **My Rating** | Your rating history. Greyed out until HR publishes it. |
| **Past Cycles** | Previous years. |
| **My Competencies** | Rate yourself against what your role needs. |
| **Timesheet** | Upload your timesheet export and see how your compliance reads. |
| **Improvement Plan** | Your improvement plan, if one is open. |
| **My Surveys** | Engagement surveys HR has sent you. |
| **People Hub** | The company noticeboard and directory. |

A menu item that is **greyed out and unclickable** is not a permissions
problem — it means there is nothing behind it yet. Hover over it and it tells
you what will open it. *My Rating*, for example, stays closed until HR
publishes your appraisal.

## 3. My KRAs — writing your goals

This is the first thing to do in a new cycle.

**Start from your role's shelf.** If HR has published a KRA Library for your
designation, click **Suggest KRAs for my role**. You get a menu of KRAs
written for your job title — pick the ones that apply. Everything you pick
stays **fully editable**: change the wording, the metric, the weight, whatever
you need. The shelf is a starting point, not a template you are locked into.

**Or write your own.** Use **Add a KRA under…** in whichever section fits
(Financial, Project / Process, Customer, People).

**Weights must total 100.** The shelf itself does not need to total 100 — it
is a menu, and you pick a hundred points' worth from it.

**Each KRA takes:**

- a title — what you are accountable for;
- a description (click **+ Add description**);
- KPIs — how it will be measured, and from what source;
- a weight — how much of your year it represents.

**Save draft** keeps your work private. **Save & submit to manager** sends it
for approval — after that your manager either approves it or returns it with a
comment asking for changes. If it comes back, the comment tells you what to
fix.

## 4. Timesheet

Two things live here: your **compliance** (did you fill your timesheet), and
your **KRA coverage** (did the work you logged line up with your goals).

### Uploading your export

1. Export your timesheet from **Zoho Sprints**.
2. Click **Choose File**, pick the file exactly as it came out of Zoho — no editing, no re-saving.
3. Click **Check the file** first if you want to see what it found.
4. Click **Upload**.

Three things worth knowing:

- **The sheet's own header row is found wherever it sits**, so extra rows above the headings are not a problem.
- **Only your own rows are loaded.** A project export usually carries the whole team; everybody else's rows are listed back to you and left alone.
- **Uploading again replaces the days the new file covers and leaves every other day untouched**, so re-uploading the same export changes nothing. If you made a correction in Zoho, just upload again.

### The Compliance tab

A calendar of the period showing which days are filled, which are short, and
which are weekends. Your compliance percentage is at the top, with a
Red / Amber / Green marker.

### The KRA coverage tab

This is where your logged hours are matched to your KRAs. Four numbers across
the top:

| Number | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Hours placed** | How much of your logged time is attached to a KRA. |
| **KRA coverage** | How much of your sheet, **by weight, not by count**, got worked on. |
| **Effort alignment** | How closely where your hours went matched the weights you agreed. |
| **Timesheet compliance** | Whether you filled your timesheet, for the same period. |

Below that, **Where the hours went** lists every KRA with the hours against
it, and **What was logged** lists every project item with the KRA it serves.

**Read this section carefully, because it is easy to misread:**

- **This screen is read-only for you.** Your manager decides which KRA each item serves. You see exactly the numbers they do — nothing is hidden from you and nothing is yours to change.
- **It does not produce a rating.** If no rating can be computed, the screen says so in a box at the top and gives the reason in plain words — for example, *"Only 72.2% of the hours considered are mapped to a KRA, below the 80% this tenant requires before a score means anything."*
- **An item can be marked "Ambiguous".** That means the words in it matched more than one of your KRAs, so the system gave the hours to **neither** and named both candidates. It is not an error and it is not a judgement. Ask your manager to place it.
- **A KRA can be marked "not measured from timesheets"**, with the reason beside it. Some goals — a customer-satisfaction score, for instance — are measured elsewhere, and the system says so rather than penalising you for logging no hours against them.
- **"No logged work against this"** on a KRA is a prompt, not an accusation. It may be entirely correct for the month.

At the bottom, **Closed months** shows the months HR has settled. A settled
month keeps the numbers it was closed with, even if a KRA is reweighted
afterwards.

**What to do if the numbers look wrong:** don't argue with the screen — it is
reporting what was logged and what was mapped. If hours are missing, the
timesheet export is the place to fix it. If hours are in the wrong place, your
manager holds the mapping.

## 5. The rest of the year

**My Growth** — your target achievements for the year, and the role you are
aiming at next. You can ask the system to draft suggestions from your KRAs;
anything it writes is **clearly labelled as a draft** and is yours to edit,
replace or delete before it counts as yours.

**Quarterly Connects** — the record of your quarterly conversations. Both you
and your manager can see and add to these.

**Mid-Year Review** — a checkpoint against each KRA at the halfway mark. You
rate and comment; your manager does the same; the overall is computed from the
per-KRA ratings, not typed in by hand.

**Annual Review** — your self-appraisal. Write your case against each KRA. The
system can draft bullets from what it knows; edit them until they are true,
because your name goes on them.

**Final Rating / My Rating** — the consolidated outcome. These stay closed
until HR publishes.

**My Competencies** — rate yourself against what your role requires. Each
competency comes with an example of what "good" looks like at your level. Your
manager rates you separately, and the two views are compared.

**My Surveys** — engagement surveys HR has sent you. **Anonymous surveys never
store your name against your answers.** That is structural in how the data is
held, not a promise in a policy document.

## 6. Common questions

**"Access denied" on a page.** You do not hold the permission for it. The
message names what is needed; send that to HR rather than guessing.

**My Rating is greyed out.** HR has not published your appraisal yet. Hovering
over the menu item says so.

**My manager changed.** Open records move with you. You do not need to redo
anything.

**Something looks empty.** Empty means empty — this system does not show
sample or placeholder data. If a screen says nothing has been set up, nothing
has been set up.

**Who do I ask?** Your manager for anything about your KRAs, your ratings or
your timesheet mapping. HR for access, passwords, cycles and published
ratings.
